Workflows

Time Tracking

Log hours in plain language. "4 hours on Nike for development." VERA records it, links it to the right project and task, and flags anything that looks off.

Projects & Budgets

Five project types — Retainer, T&M, Fixed Price, Pro Bono, and Agile. Each has its own billing model and VERA handles the math.

Allocations & Capacity

Managers assign hour envelopes to team members. VERA tracks plan vs. actual and surfaces overbooking before it becomes a problem.

Financial Intelligence

Revenue recognition, cost calculation, margin analysis — all live, all in Slack. Owners see dollars; Managers see percentages.

Roles & Permissions

Three roles with clearly scoped access. Owners see everything. Managers see their projects. Users see their own time.

Reporting Sheet

A separate Google Sheet with 11 dashboard tabs, auto-populated from the database via IMPORTRANGE. No bot access needed.
