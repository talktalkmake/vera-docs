Owner & manager reference

Just say it

Tell VERA what you worked on and they record it. No forms, no dropdowns, no end-of-week scramble. Plain language, whenever it suits you.

Your morning briefing

Every weekday morning, VERA sends a personalised summary — your hours, your work plan, and (for managers) your portfolio health.

Time off, handled

Request holiday, sick days, and leave through VERA. Your manager is notified, approves or declines, and your availability updates automatically.

Three roles, clear boundaries

Team members see their own time. Managers see their projects. Owners see everything including financials.
