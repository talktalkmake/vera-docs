Stop checking time entries. Start judging results. ​
You can't run an agency by reading timesheets. VERA gives your project managers the numbers and the authority to run their projects, and gives you the results of how they did.
A live P&L, not a month-end project
Revenue, cost, contribution margin and labor efficiency by month, client and discipline, current as of the last hour recorded.
Your whole business on one screen
What you made last month, which projects need attention and who's over or under this week, in one view and again in your morning message.
What you stop doing ​
  • Reading timesheets to find problems. Each project says in plain words what it needs, and only what needs you reaches you.
  • Being the only one who knows if a project makes money. Your project managers see each project's labor efficiency as they run it, without seeing anyone's pay.
  • Signing off every correction. A project manager fixes the hours budget, the deadline and misplaced time on their own project. A moved deadline stays on the record.
What you judge instead ​
Compare your project managers in one table: labor efficiency, projects on course, how accurate their plans were, how well deadlines held, and profit. The measure is the outcome, not the entries.
Pay rates stay with you, and project managers never see them.
Run your projects without asking permission. ​
You answer for how a project turns out, so you should have the numbers and the authority to steer it. VERA gives you both, while there's still time to act.
See what each project needs next
Every hour recorded updates the project. The burn-up shows pace against your plan, and VERA says in plain words what the project needs next.
Know who's free before you say yes
A week-by-week capacity view built from everyone's allocations, time off and holidays, so you can answer a start date while the client is still on the call.
What you can do on your own projects ​
  • Correct or move anyone's time, and fix the hours budget and the deadline.
  • Plan each person's hours week by week, and see who is delivering to the plan.
  • Add a fixed fee for a vendor or contractor, and roll a retainer into its next period.
  • Hand a project to another manager when things change.
What you're judged on ​
Your projects' results: labor efficiency, staying on course, how accurate your plans were and how well your deadlines held. You see each project's labor efficiency as you go, so nothing arrives as a surprise at month end.
