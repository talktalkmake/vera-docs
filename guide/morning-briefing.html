Your daily view ​

VERA is built around timeliness — seeing where things stand now, not at the end of the month. The console gives you an at-a-glance read whenever you open it. What you see depends on your role.

For team members ​

Open Time. The list shows your recent entries, so a few seconds tells you whether everything's logged and nothing's slipped. Log the day's work as you go and your record stays current.

Check Time Off for the status of any holiday or leave you've requested, and Calendar for upcoming company holidays that affect your week.

For managers and owners ​

The console opens on your portfolio — every project you're responsible for, with at-a-glance burn and health, so you can scan for anything drifting before it becomes a problem. From there:

Envelopes — who's allocated to what, with logged-vs-remaining for each person, so you can see where capacity is tight or slack.

Capacity — a forward view of how booked your team is across the coming weeks.

Approvals — time-off requests waiting on you.

Unresolved — items VERA has flagged that need a decision or action.

Reports — estimates vs actuals, and (owners) the full financial picture: revenue recognized, cost, and margin.

Projects that are off the plan line — over or under by a meaningful margin — surface at the top with clear status flags, so the things most worth your attention find you rather than the other way round.
