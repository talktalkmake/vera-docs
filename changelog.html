Changelog ​

New in VERA, week by week: what you can now do, and how it helps. The newest week is first, and weeks with nothing new are left out. Link to a single week with its anchor, for example changelog.html#week-36.

Week 41 · Nov 16, 2026 ​

Owners ​

  • See one person's week on Activity: pick them from the person filter and the days, the total and the entries narrow to them.
  • Record each project's gross profit, the fee less costs VERA doesn't track, so labor efficiency and the P&L reflect what your team's time earns.
  • See VERA exactly as one of your managers or team members does: open their page and choose View as. Nothing can be changed while you look.
  • See how your finished projects' estimates held up: the Estimates tab splits them into under, on and over budget.
  • Switch the home page's Projects and People tiles between their groups, and open a whole group with Show more.
  • See your projects worst LER first: the projects table now opens ordered by labor efficiency, lowest at the top, so the projects losing the most money are the first thing you see.
  • Require a note on every time entry on a project, so each hour you bill or review says what it was for.
  • See whether each project finished by its original deadline as its own part of the grade, so finishing on time counts, and moving a deadline doesn't hide a late finish.

Project managers ​

  • Read a person's whole week in one card on their page: the pace to finish on each allocation, and every allocation shown, even one with nothing planned this week.
  • See and change the gross profit and target LER of every project you manage, and each retainer period's gross profit.
  • Give each person one allocation per project: every hour they record on any of its tasks counts toward it.
  • See on your home page only the allocations that might need a look, each with the reason and a small chart of plan against recorded hours; a line under the card's title says what puts an allocation under each filter.
  • See where each allocation's person will land at their current pace, with a warning once 20% of the project's time has passed and the pace runs more than 10% over.
  • See each person's week at a glance: the This week card shows their status beside its title and what their allocations ask of their week, with a gray "as expected" until a day's hours are due.
  • See when a project's hours budget is bigger than its allocations add up to, what that does to its LER, and a link straight to the budget; and, on a running Fixed Price project, the most LER it can reach at its allocations and at the current pace, and the hours its target still allows, beside its gross profit in Financials.
  • Follow one person's allocation on its own page: a burn-up, its schedule and when its hours run out at the current pace, with the time entries one tab away.

Everyone ​

  • Log time straight from each line of This week on My time: pick the date, the task (already set to your usual one) and the hours.
  • See why a week holds fewer hours: This week notes time away and company holidays after the hours available.
  • See each allocation's last week, this week and total side by side, each in red when it falls behind.
  • Tell at a glance whether each week and allocation is on track, slipping or off track, from a green, amber or red dot and how many hours it is out.
  • See on My time where you'll land on each of your allocations at your current pace.
  • Find the current week at a glance on This week: it's tinted by its status, and a week before an allocation began shows as not started.
  • Switch workspaces from a popover in the sidebar, so you never leave the page you're on.

Week 40 · Nov 9, 2026 ​

Owners ​

  • Read the forecast's highest cumulative revenue straight off the chart's axis, marked with a line at its height.
  • Mark a proposal won or lost with the Status button (two presses, so never by accident), and filter proposals by draft, won or lost, newest first.
  • Tick a retainer proposal in the forecast's scenario to see its fee added every month you're looking at.
  • See every project graded on the Project Managers tab, running and finished, and each project manager's own grade weighted by the size of their projects.
  • Open each project manager's report card: their grade from finished projects and a grade so far for running ones, with a project that changed hands starred and counted for nobody.
  • Choose how many weeks, months, quarters or years your P&L shows; it opens on the last 12 months.
  • Propose a retainer, priced by its fee and hours per period, and convert it into a retainer starting on the date you choose.
  • Connect your own budgeting tool or calculator to VERA with a key: it reads each project's labor cost at finish, with the fixed fees beside it.

Project managers ​

  • Open your own report card from the bottom of the sidebar to see the grade your projects earn you and which projects count toward it.
  • See everyone named on a proposal on the Capacity heatmap, each week showing their work with the proposal added.
  • See in a proposal's cost breakdown whether each person named has room, is tight or is full, with each line's weekly plan beside its hours.
  • See the date each week starts along the bottom of an allocation's hours chart.
  • Type a week's hours straight into an allocation's hours chart, quarter hours like 7.25 included.
  • Add a vendor yourself from Staffing → Vendors, and set up their fixed fee without waiting for an owner.
  • Open a person's page to see their hours on every project, with each one's share and the last day they recorded.
  • Read a project's allocations one person at a time, each showing how many they hold and their hours across all of them.

Everyone ​

  • Find a person's planned hours on a project called an allocation everywhere in VERA: the screens, emails and Slack all say the same thing.
  • See your own hours on every project at the bottom of My time.
  • Point anywhere over a week on a chart to see every figure for that week in one tooltip.
  • See hours planned for earlier weeks and not yet recorded added to this week on My time and in capacity.
  • See each of your allocations as one line on My time: this week's hours recorded against what's due, and how far through the whole allocation you are against its plan.
  • Tell planned hours from recorded hours on a project's burn-up and Delivery charts: recorded in violet, planned in a lighter, dashed blue.

Week 39 · Nov 2, 2026 ​

Owners ​

  • Start a new workspace with Design, Development, Project Management, Admin and Meeting tasks already set as billable or not and linked to their disciplines, and each new project with the right ones enabled.
  • Read your P&L by month, quarter or year.

  • See each vendor's fixed-fee hours per week and their time away below the team on Capacity.
  • Filter a project's time entries by task as well as by person, with the total following the filter.
  • Download a client-facing estimate from any proposal: scope, hours and the total, never your costs or rates.
  • Tick time entries on a project and move them to one allocation.
  • Read your P&L by week for the last 12 weeks, and see a slow week while there's still time to act.

Project managers ​

  • Open any retainer period to see its own burn-up, delivery, time entries and whether it's on course.
  • Switch a retainer's burn-up to any one period from a picker above the chart.
  • Open a project's Audit tab to see who created it and who changed its budget, PM, deadline, allocations and fees, and when.
  • See a long-running retainer's 12 newest periods first, with Load more for older ones.
  • Show only your full-time staff or only your contractors on Capacity.
  • Open Capacity on your full-time staff, with the people booked just right at the top and contractors one click away.
  • Keep notes on what each vendor is like to work with on Staffing → Vendors, hidden until you click.
  • Open time recorded outside allocations entry by entry, and jump to each one, highlighted, on the person's time page.

Everyone ​

  • See the allocations you can work on now on My time, with upcoming and finished retainer periods in their own card.
  • Read what VERA makes of every finished project: the money, the time, and whether planning or chance earned the result.
  • See every finished project graded A to F, overall and for its money, its time and how it was run.
  • See every running project's grade so far, and sort your projects by it.
  • See what a running project's grade so far is made of on its page: its money, its time and how it's run, each with its letter and the reason.
  • See the LER on every project as a manager, not only the ones you run.

Week 37 · Oct 19, 2026 ​

Owners ​

  • See how your pay turns into profit the way Greg Crabtree measures it: direct, management and total LER and contribution margin for the last 12 months, with project LER and utilization showing why direct LER moved. Contribution margin counts all your delivery team's pay and leads the Money tile on your home page.
  • Invoice from VERA: set who invoices come from and each client's billing contact and terms, build lines from recorded time, a billing schedule month or a retainer period, and download a PDF with your logo.
  • Connect your own assistant app to VERA to draft proposals: it reads your past projects, people and costs, prices the work at your target LER, checks everyone named has the time, and leaves a draft for you to review.
  • Make a manager Manager Plus: they see the Money and Performance hubs, every project's LER and each person's pay, and manage a project's money, without owner powers.
  • Set your company's headquarters in Settings, so salary bands refresh from your region's wage data.
  • See everyone's hours week by week on Contributions, and on Recorded hours how each person's recorded time compares with the hours they're paid for, with any shortfall split into planned but not recorded and nothing planned.
  • See what each task on an ongoing internal project has cost beside its hours, and give the project a monthly hours budget.
  • Bring your Harvest history in one project at a time, with a progress bar, and come back days later without losing an answer. Merge a duplicate project into the right one after a preview.

Project managers ​

  • See which tasks from Asana, Jira and your other tools an allocation's hours went on, with the hours for each.
  • See the hours behind every percentage on the capacity heatmap; Capacity, the People tile and the morning summary all count hours already recorded this week.
  • See on a new project's Financials card what it still needs, such as a billing rate, an hours budget or an allocation.
  • Rename the projects you manage, and hand one to another manager from Edit project.
  • Give each reference on an allocation a title: links to Asana, Jira, Linear, GitHub and other tools show the tool's logo and a readable name.
  • See how a person's week stands at a glance: done, planned and free against their capacity, with today marked.
  • Switch your project list to all projects, to help with a colleague's project while they're away.
  • See each month's billable and non-billable hours by project and person on the Hours tab, and download them.

Everyone ​

  • Open your allocation's task links from My time in one click; time you log to it carries those links. Connect your own Asana account so linked tasks show their real names.
  • Get a morning edition each working day: what you recorded yesterday and how this week's plan is going on each allocation.
  • Get your scheduled reminders by email whenever VERA has your email address, without needing Slack.
  • See on each allocation the hours planned for you this week and how many you've recorded, with one line saying whether you're ahead, on track or behind.
  • See what your allocations ask of you each week against your normal week.
  • See which internal projects run indefinitely and which have a deadline and budget in the Internal view of the projects list; every list that names a client puts the client first.
  • Reach your clients from the Projects page, on a Clients tab beside your projects.
  • Move a time entry to another project from its Edit form; a project manager can move many at once from a project's Time entries tab after a preview.
