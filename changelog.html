Changelog ​

What has changed in VERA, week by week. The newest week is first.

Week 36 · Oct 12, 2026 ​

  • Project managers: Attach links or notes to an envelope to say which pieces of work count toward it.
  • Project managers: See monthly hours by project and by person on Staffing, hours only, with a CSV download.
  • Project managers: Record a vendor's time away so planning shows when they are unavailable.
  • Owners: An invite email is sent when you add a person, and a Send invite button covers anyone added earlier.
  • Owners: Import your history from Harvest in the console, with safe re-runs and one-click undo, then top up later time.
  • Owners: Warnings appear when someone with an allocation has no cost rate.
  • Owners: LER is measured as gross margin over direct labor, and contractor and vendor fixed fees are treated alike.
  • Project managers: Move a project to another client, see a project's client, type and deadline in its header, and see how well deadlines are held.

Week 35 · Oct 5, 2026 ​

  • Project managers: Edit a retainer period on its own page, and attach vendor liabilities to a period.
  • Owners: Contractors on a fixed price can hold a liability that accrues by the calendar and moves together with their envelope.
  • Project managers: A Vendors tab on Staffing lists every vendor and contractor with their liabilities.
  • Project managers: Resolve or dismiss several unresolved items at once, and hours fields accept two decimal places.
  • Owners: A vendor's accrued hours count as earned revenue in the Monthly P&L and Billing.
  • Team members: Time is recorded only by the person who did it; a project manager can edit any entry on their project.
  • Project managers: Envelope pages show each week's number, and burn-up charts run on through planned retainer periods.
  • Everyone: Vendors can no longer sign in to the console, and time entries without a task read as a dash.

Week 34 · Sep 28, 2026 ​

  • Everyone: The console is reorganized into hubs: Money, Staffing, Needs you, Performance, Settings and Logs, each with tabs.
  • Project managers: The home page opens with a one-screen brief of money, projects and people, and the morning message uses the same brief.
  • Project managers: A retainer is judged on its current period, and a due renewal shows on its page.
  • Project managers: Duplicate an envelope, see time outside envelopes, and list a project's tasks alphabetically.
  • Owners: Convert a project between types.
  • Project managers: The Needs you page lists everything waiting, including projects that still need setup, and unresolved items are grouped by type.
  • Team members: A week in progress counts only the days left in it, so capacity reads correctly mid-week.
  • Everyone: PTO, Sick and Leave are evergreen: no dates, budgets or financials.

Week 33 · Sep 21, 2026 ​

  • Project managers: Retainers can be created again: each period has its own page for staffing, edits and tasks, with a Re-up control to roll forward.
  • Project managers: The PM of a retainer is told when a period is about to end with no successor.
  • Project managers: Past capacity weeks show recorded hours, and finished projects stop counting as committed.
  • Owners: Forecast scenarios show the effect of winning an open proposal alongside the committed forecast.
  • Owners: The projects table has an LER column, and a completed T&M project's unapproved time is back in the approvals queue.
  • Project managers: Configuration, PM and Enabled tasks are one card, and advice links open the form they point to.
  • Everyone: Everyday labels are clearer: for example "103h over" instead of a negative figure.

Week 32 · Sep 14, 2026 ​

  • Project managers: A "What this project needs" card lists the next steps for each project in plain language, ranked by what clears the most.
  • Project managers: Set a project's start date; time before a client project starts is refused, and envelope windows stay inside the project's dates.
  • Owners: Set a company-wide target LER and override it per project, with a button that sets hours for the target.
  • Owners: A Finish setting up checklist on the home page walks a new workspace through the basics.
  • Project managers: Managers can create projects and clients and make themselves the PM of a project without one.
  • Owners: Use VERA without Slack, signing in with Google or Microsoft, and move your own account between owner and admin.
  • Everyone: The morning briefing reads correctly by email, and time reads answer for exactly the person you ask about.
  • Everyone: Tightened who can see and change what.

Week 31 · Sep 7, 2026 ​

  • Owners: Project cost no longer applies an employer cost multiplier; that figure now lives in the Users hub only.
  • Project managers: See how each allocation's plan changed over time, and a Plan accuracy column on the Project Managers tab.
  • Everyone: Full-time staff are automatically given holiday time once a company holiday has passed.
  • Everyone: Dates follow your workspace's own time zone.
  • Owners: Gross pay and employer cost on the Direct labor tab are calculated the right way round.
  • Everyone: Chart axes spell out months in full and show fewer labels on long schedules.

Week 30 · Aug 31, 2026 ​

  • Owners: A month-end Billing view compares what each project was scheduled to bill with what it earned.
  • Owners: Fixed Price projects can carry a billing schedule, edited on its own page.
  • Project managers: T&M projects have a time approval page where a PM writes hours down before billing.
  • Project managers: Reset to default and Undo reset on both shaping editors.
  • Owners: Discipline-level fixes: tasks are classified for client or internal work, and by-discipline reports no longer misattribute time.

Week 29 · Aug 24, 2026 ​

  • Project managers: The envelopes table is narrower and easier to read, with a sortable Hours column and a usage bar that reads each envelope on its own.
  • Owners: Contributions shows each figure as a percentage of capacity, and chart lines match each person's color.
  • Project managers: Task notes appear on hover wherever a task is listed.
  • Project managers: Managers can open a capacity cell, and the envelope chart shows other commitments and weekly capacity as context.
  • Project managers: Budget health and Projected exhaustion show the working behind them.

Week 28 · Aug 17, 2026 ​

  • Project managers: Log time to a specific allocation when more than one covers the same task; capacity no longer double-counts hours across them.
  • Owners: Every scheduled message VERA sends is recorded and can be read by an owner.
  • Project managers: The Schedule verdict shows its working: how many hours were recorded against the plan, and the plan's expected progress.
  • Owners: Export a CSV for a project or a date range, with cost columns and no blank or truncated rows.
  • Project managers: Allocations can carry a label, and the Log time dropdown shows each project's client.
  • Owners: People filters can separate contractors and vendors.

Week 27 · Aug 10, 2026 ​

  • Team members: Time, Activity and Time off are now one tabbed Time page.
  • Project managers: Charts on Reports, Forecast and Portfolio can be zoomed.
  • Project managers: The Activity tab shows company holidays and the work week.

Week 26 · Aug 3, 2026 ​

  • Project managers: A Delivery view shows planned versus recorded hours per person, week by week, with pills ranked by how far off plan each person is.
  • Owners: Disciplines carry a color shown across the console, and tables sort by clicking any header.
  • Project managers: Charts can be zoomed with the mouse wheel or buttons and panned by dragging.
  • Team members: The Time page marks holidays, weekends, time off and empty days, and shows the week total beside what is expected.
  • Project managers: Time recorded on a company holiday is flagged for review instead of blocked.
  • Everyone: Dates read as "Jul 30", success messages float and fade, and errors stay on screen until fixed.
  • Owners: The Budget health label clarifies what it measures, and the contribution-margin column uses a segmented bar.
  • Everyone: Everything is written in American English.

Week 25 · Jul 27, 2026 ​

  • Project managers: Shape an allocation across the weeks it covers, and see the plan as a Planned pace line on the burn-up chart; on-course status follows your plan.
  • Project managers: Capacity honors the window and weekly shape of each allocation; a proposal's capacity simulation shows demand week by week.
  • Project managers: Envelopes are edited on their own page with a readable chart.
  • Owners: Users, Rates, Bands and Direct labor became one tabbed Users page; expand a person to see their rate and level history.
  • Project managers: Time outside any allocation, or beyond an allocation's hours, becomes a short exception list instead of an approval step.
  • Owners: Projected exhaustion follows the plan, and the portfolio uses the same planned baseline as each project.
  • Everyone: Charts are smoother and no longer depend on outside libraries.

Week 24 · Jul 20, 2026 ​

  • Owners: A team Contributions view compares everyone's hours by month, quarter or year, with filters, bulk select and a per-person view.
  • Owners: Compensation bands per discipline and level, a direct-labor view that compares pay to the band, and salary bands refreshed from published wage data.
  • Project managers: The capacity heatmap uses a scale where idle and overbooked are both red, and grays out weeks with no availability.
  • Project managers: Managers see a My projects group in the sidebar, a list scoped to their own projects and a "You" badge.
  • Everyone: Link-style navigation updates part of the page in place instead of reloading.
  • Everyone: Every sidebar item has an icon; the audit channel now announces only things that are created or retroactive time changes.
  • Everyone: Admin accounts no longer get time-recording nudges, and LER excludes projects that earn no revenue.

Week 23 · Jul 13, 2026 ​

  • Everyone: Sessions renew automatically for 30 days of use instead of expiring after 12 hours.
  • Project managers: The capacity heatmap and the person page agree on overbooked weeks, and Projected exhaustion shows the gap to the deadline in work days.
  • Project managers: Names in week-entry tables link to the person and project, and the person time page opens to project managers.

Week 22 · Jul 6, 2026 ​

  • Everyone: Behind-the-scenes reliability and speed improvements.

Week 21 · Jun 29, 2026 ​

  • Owners: Closed proposals are locked read-only, and the hours budget is suggested from contract value, target LER and blended cost.
  • Everyone: The console works better on a phone: tables scroll within themselves and page margins tighten.
  • Owners: Every new project automatically starts with Project Management enabled.
  • Everyone: A round of fixes for financial calculations, input checks and reminder timing.

Week 20 · Jun 22, 2026 ​

  • Owners: Proposals let you model a prospective engagement: staffing, costs, a vendor make-or-buy view, a capacity simulation and one-click conversion to a project when won.
  • Owners: A forecasted P&L panel and a project cost breakdown show each person's share of cost.
  • Project managers: A team activity feed and a read-only person time page show who recorded what.
  • Project managers: The capacity heatmap can slide forward and back by week, with a by-discipline summary.

Week 19 · Jun 15, 2026 ​

  • Owners: Reports gained tabs: monthly P&L, estimates versus actuals, a CSV export, and Segmentation by customer and by discipline with make-or-buy LER.
  • Owners: Manage tasks, disciplines, settings, company holidays, time off and unresolved items from the console.
  • Project managers: See a capacity heatmap across the team, a portfolio chart of margin versus schedule, and sparklines in the project list.
  • Project managers: Project pages show a burn-up chart, work days remaining and a live budget panel for Fixed Price projects.
  • Team members: Request time off yourself, with a notice-period picker that blocks dates too soon, and log time against a default task for your discipline.
  • Owners: Cost-aware hours-budget calculator warns when a price implies a loss.
  • Everyone: Rates now follow the correct effective date, and amounts in the audit channel are hidden.

  • Owners: Console navigation became a left sidebar with mobile support, modal forms and a workspace switcher for multi-workspace email identities.
  • Owners: Sign in with Google or Microsoft, and delete empty clients and projects.
  • Project managers: A top-level Envelopes page lists every allocation with person filter, sorting and usage dot.
  • Team members: A Time page lets you log, review and edit your own hours.
