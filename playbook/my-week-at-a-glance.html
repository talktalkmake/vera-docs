My week at a glance ​

Cadence: Weekly (Monday morning) · Who: Everyone

Situation ​

It's Monday. You want to see what's on your plate this week — which projects, how many hours you're allocated, what you've logged so far, and what's left to do.

This is also delivered automatically in the morning briefing, but you can ask for it any time.

Example phrasings ​

  • "what am I working on this week"
  • "my allocations"
  • "where's my time going this week"

What VERA returns ​

A table of your active allocations, one row per envelope:

| Project | Task | Allocated | Logged | Remaining |

Plus:

  • Any time off you've already got approved this week
  • Deadlines coming up in the next 7 days, if any
  • A one-line summary: total allocated hours, total logged, total remaining

Sorted by remaining hours descending, so the heaviest unstarted work is at the top.

Who can run it ​

Everyone. Returns your own allocations only.
